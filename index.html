    ABOUT ME

    Six years in the classroom opened my eyes to one recurring problem: we have no real visibility into how students think. So I built EdCipher, a math test prep tool with integrated computer-adaptive testing, designed to decipher what each student needs next while closing the Cognitive Accountability Triangle between students, teachers, and parents.
