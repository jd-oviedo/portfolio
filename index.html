    ABOUT ME

    Five years in the classroom opened my eyes to one recurring problem: we have almost no visibility into how students think. So I built EdCipher Math — an AI platform built on the Cognitive Accountability Triangle, where students capture their reasoning, teachers diagnose misconceptions, and parents get plain-language insight. Same data, three views, one feedback loop.
